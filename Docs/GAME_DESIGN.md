# MORABEZA — Game Design Foundation

## 1. Vision

MORABEZA is an original open-world game inspired by Cape Verdean life. The player explores a living island, builds relationships, earns money, makes choices and can pursue legitimate, entrepreneurial, social or criminal paths.

The game should feel specifically Cape Verdean through its geography, language, architecture, transport, music, social behavior and economic context.

## 2. First World

### Santiago — Praia Vertical Slice

The initial world is a fictionalized, geographically inspired version of Praia and its surrounding environment. The first slice will not attempt to reproduce the whole island.

Priority environments:

- Urban streets
- Residential neighborhoods
- Commercial area
- Coastal area
- Hills and viewpoints
- Main roads
- Small rural transition area

## 3. Player Loop

```text
Explore
  ↓
Meet people / discover opportunities
  ↓
Take a job, mission or activity
  ↓
Earn money / reputation / relationships
  ↓
Upgrade player, vehicle or property
  ↓
Unlock new opportunities
  ↓
Explore further
```

## 4. Player Attributes

Initial systems:

- Money
- Reputation
- Relationships
- Inventory
- Current vehicle
- Mission state
- World progression

## 5. World Systems

The world will eventually contain:

- Pedestrians
- Traffic
- Police response
- Shops
- Businesses
- Public spaces
- Weather
- Day/night cycle
- Dynamic events
- Factions
- Economic simulation

## 6. Cultural Direction

The game may use Portuguese and Cape Verdean Creole in context. Dialogue, signage, music and environmental storytelling should be developed with cultural accuracy and reviewed by Cape Verdean contributors where possible.

Real-world people, brands and institutions should not be represented as fictional criminal organizations or otherwise mischaracterized without appropriate rights and permissions.

## 7. First Mission

Working title: **Volta**

The player arrives in Praia, meets an acquaintance and receives an introductory task that teaches movement, interaction, navigation and the game's basic economy.

The mission must be small enough to prove the core loop while establishing the setting.

## 8. M0.1 Definition of Done

- Game launches into a playable level
- Player can move in third person
- Camera follows player
- Player can enter a prototype vehicle
- Vehicle can be driven
- At least one NPC can be approached
- NPC interaction triggers a mission
- Mission can be completed
- Player receives money
- Game state can be saved
- Game state can be loaded

## 9. Future Expansion

After M0.1, the project can expand into:

- Larger Praia map
- Additional Santiago locations
- More vehicles
- NPC schedules
- Police system
- Businesses
- Property
- Factions
- Advanced mission branching
- Audio/radio
- Multiplayer/backend experiments
- Additional Cape Verde islands
